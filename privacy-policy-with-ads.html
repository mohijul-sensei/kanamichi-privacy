KanaMichi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

KanaMichi (かな道) is a Hiragana and Katakana learning app. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect or store personal information on our own servers, and we have no accounts or sign-in. Third-party advertising is described below.

Data stored on your device

Your learning progress, scores, bookmarks and settings (language, pronunciation mode, theme, daily goal) are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. You can export a backup or delete everything at any time from Settings → Your data.

Microphone and speech recognition

The Pronunciation Challenge game asks for microphone permission. It is optional. When you use it, your device or browser's speech-recognition service (for example Google's speech service in Chrome/Android) may process your voice to check your pronunciation. KanaMichi itself does not record or keep any audio.

Advertising

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device advertising ID, IP address and app usage information to show and measure ads, and, where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), asks for your consent first through Google's consent form. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See Google's advertising policy and Privacy Policy.

Audio

Character sounds are played using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Fonts

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts when you are online, which means your device contacts Google's font servers. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app shows a local notification at the time you choose. No data leaves your device.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. Ads are not personalised for users who have not given consent where consent is required.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
